Block 1 Evidence and Debrief Aid

Flight Test New Zealand Aviation Examiner Recurrent Professional Development

Use this aid to structure a difficult or mixed-evidence assessment. It supports professional reasoning and does not replace the applicable assessment standard or FTNZ process.
