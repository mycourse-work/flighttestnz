# Block Review and Completion

Block 1 has focused on the examiner’s continuing responsibility to remain current, independent and evidence-driven during difficult live assessments.

## Key points

- Experience strengthens judgement only when it remains anchored to evidence and current standards.
- Professional independence matters most when familiarity, operational need or pressure is present.
- Assessment conduct requires disciplined observation, workload management and careful interpretation of errors, recovery and emerging patterns.
- An error should be judged in context; recognition and recovery add evidence but do not erase the original event.
- Borderline and mixed evidence should be separated into observation, judgement and decision.
- Professional debriefing should explain outcomes using observable evidence and standards.
- Calibration depends on making reasoning visible, not simply agreeing on an outcome.

## Final check before the quiz

Ask yourself:

1. Can I identify what was observed before describing what it means?
2. Can I separate a safety intervention from the assessment significance of that intervention?
3. Can I name the effect of operational pressure without allowing it to change the standard?
4. Can I explain both the original event and any recovery?
5. Could another experienced examiner reconstruct my reasoning from my notes?

Complete the Block 1 knowledge check to record your understanding. It is a formative knowledge check; FTNZ has not specified a formal pass mark in the supplied source. Review and retry any questions that need attention.

The [optional Block 1 evidence and debrief aid](./downloads/block-1-evidence-and-debrief-aid.md) condenses the five-pass review for later field use; it is not an additional mandatory lesson.

## Completion position

The objective of Block 1 is not to create identical examiners. It is to maintain examiners who apply current standards consistently, protect assessment integrity, communicate clearly and can defend difficult decisions using evidence.

> **TEM Reflection:** What threat, error or undesired outcome will you actively monitor for in your next assessment, and what control will you use?
