## Block 2 — Readiness, Human Performance and Calibration

Welcome to Block 2 of the Flight Test New Zealand (FTNZ) Aviation Examiner Recurrent Professional Development Programme.

This block is for experienced FTNZ aviation examiners. It focuses on whether an assessment is genuinely ready to proceed and what observed behaviour demonstrates about competence. It assumes you already understand Threat and Error Management (TEM), human factors, NOTECHS and assessment preparation.

## What you will practise

- Separating likely competence from assessment legitimacy.
- Evaluating readiness, prerequisites and cumulative threats.
- Making a considered go/no-go decision under pressure.
- Moving from observable behaviour to a supported competence judgement.
- Weighing errors and recovery as one complete evidence sequence.
- Recognising halo, horn, confirmation, recency and expectation effects.
- Calibrating mixed human-performance evidence against the applicable standard.

## How to work through the block

Read each lesson, complete the reflective prompts, and finish the knowledge check. The calibration workshop asks you to commit to your reasoning before comparing it with FTNZ model reasoning. The purpose is professional development and standardisation, not speed.

> **Important:** This programme complements, and does not replace, Civil Aviation Rules, Advisory Circulars, examiner delegations or approvals, FTNZ exposition requirements, approved assessment standards, flight-test guides or other authoritative material. Current regulatory and procedural currency remains your ongoing responsibility.

## Recurrent cycle

FTNZ uses three standalone recurrent blocks across a six-year cycle. Your assigned block is determined by FTNZ. Block 1 and Block 3 are separate course entries and become available only when FTNZ assigns them for your cycle.

## Completion records

The portal records your learning progress and completion date. Follow FTNZ’s enrolment and administration process for the participant details and CAA participation ID required for the organisation’s training record.
